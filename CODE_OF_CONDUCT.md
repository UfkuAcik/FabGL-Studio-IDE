# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in FabGL Studio welcoming and harassment-free for everyone,
regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and
expression, experience, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, appearance, race,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, specific, and constructive.
- Assume good intent while addressing technical evidence directly.
- Accept responsibility, apologize, and repair harm when mistakes occur.
- Keep private information private.
- Focus criticism on code, design, evidence, and behavior—not people.

Harassment, threats, sexualized conduct, discriminatory language, deliberate intimidation,
doxxing, and sustained disruption are unacceptable.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit, reject, or remove contributions and temporarily or permanently
restrict participation when conduct threatens a safe, productive community. Reports should
be sent privately to the security/contact address published by the repository host. Reports
will be handled as confidentially as practical, with a fair opportunity for affected parties
to provide context.

This policy is adapted from Contributor Covenant 2.1. The authoritative text is available at
https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct.html.
